Speaker detection model (Yascribe: Settings -> Speaker detection)

The model is not part of this app. You download it with the Download button in Settings.
It is stored on this Mac and used only for speaker detection.

Creators (attribution under CC BY 4.0):
- pyannote (Herve Bredin and Alexis Plaquet) - pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1
  https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1
- WeSpeaker (Hongji Wang et al.) - speaker embedding
- BUT Speech@FIT, Brno University of Technology - PLDA parameters and VBx clustering
  (Landini, Profant, Diez, Burget)
- Fluid Inference - Core ML conversion

Source of the files:
  https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml
  revision df2625ac79a7ac6b65ad868fee6d80f320da4232

Changes: these files are modified Core ML conversions of the original PyTorch models
(fixed and enumerated input shapes, mixed-precision storage, FBank frontend separated
from the embedding backend, compiled for Apple platforms), made by Fluid Inference.
They are not fine-tuned models.
